Fixed-width fields are how the format keeps every record lined up, and the doc's constraints say text fields are left-justified and filled with spaces.

You can check a line by counting its parts:

00, then a space (3 characters).
User001 plus 8 spaces, which is 15 characters, then a space (16 characters).
FS, then a space (3 characters).
000100.00, which is 9 characters.
That makes 31 characters for the whole line. If a tester or the Front End uses a different username width, every expected file would break, so fix 15 as your standard and note it in the test plan.
